# Table 4. Authentication criteria for salivary gland cancer patient-derived organoids

| Criterion | Recommended methods | Assessment and reporting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Histomorphology | Hematoxylin and eosin (H&E) staining; side-by-side comparison with the parental tumor. | Compare characteristic architecture, cytology and subtype-defining patterns. Document discrepancies and sampling limitations. |
| Subtype-defining driver alterations | Reverse-transcription PCR, fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH), or fusion-capable sequencing, as appropriate. | Confirm retention of alterations identified in the parental tumor (e.g., MYB–NFIB / MYBL1–NFIB in ACC, CRTC1/3–MAML2 in MEC, or ETV6–NTRK3 in secretory carcinoma). |
| Mutation and copy-number profile | Targeted DNA sequencing, whole-exome sequencing, and/or copy-number analysis according to tumor subtype and available tissue. | Assess concordance with informative parental-tumor alterations, including amplifications and copy-number changes; report newly detected or lost alterations. |
| Transcriptomic fidelity | RNA sequencing and/or focused expression profiling when relevant to the study purpose. | Assess preservation of lineage- and tumor-associated transcriptional programs; report differences with the parental tumor, where matched data exist. |
| Passage history and stability | Record passage number; repeat selected morphology and molecular tests after prolonged expansion or before functional screening. | Specify passage numbers at authentication and drug testing; document culture-associated drift or clonal selection and reassessment intervals. |
| Tumor identity and culture purity | Subtype-appropriate immunohistochemistry / immunofluorescence, morphology and molecular confirmation. | Distinguish neoplastic epithelium from residual normal ductal, basal/myoepithelial and stromal populations; document contamination or overgrowth. |

Assay selection should be tailored to tumor subtype, intended use and the defining alterations present in the matched parental specimen. No single sequencing modality is mandatory for every culture.

Abbreviations: ACC, adenoid cystic carcinoma; MEC, mucoepidermoid carcinoma; PCR, polymerase chain reaction; FISH, fluorescence in situ hybridization.
