| Tumor subtype | Histology | Molecular features | Relevant pathways | Candidate therapy /target | PDO evidence / limitation | Refs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mucoepidermoid carcinoma (MEC) | Squamoid, mucous, intermediate cells | CRTC1/3–MAML2 fusion | CREB-associated transcription; autocrine AREG–EGFR axis | EGFR-directed agents (proposed) | MEC PDOs reported; EGFR-targeted screening remains a proposed use, not direct PDO evidence. | 7,17,101,104–106,113 |
