| RESTART / University Medical Center Groningen (Netherlands) | Autologous salivary organoid-derived cell transplantation for radiation-associated salivary dysfunction after oral cavity cancer surgery and postoperative (chemo)radiotherapy. | Patient-derived cells expanded ex vivo. | Mayo Clinic salivary tissue–organoid biorepository (USA) | Clinically annotated research resource supporting regenerative medicine and disease modeling; not a therapeutic intervention. | Salivary tissue and organoid biorepository established from 208 donors; used for progenitor characterization and assay development. | Established biorepository resource. Supports future translational research but does not provide transplantation safety or efficacy outcomes. | 23 |

RESTART registration and treatment details describe a prospective trial and its published manufacturing process; they should not be interpreted as evidence of clinical efficacy. The 5 × 10⁴–5 × 10⁶ cells/vial range is a manufacturing specification, not a verified administered dose.

ATORM-S development stage is based on company disclosures, whereas transplantation efficacy data cited for this program remain preclinical. References shown here retain the current manuscript/source identifiers and will be reconciled with the final bibliography.

Abbreviations: SG, salivary gland; GMP, good manufacturing practice; SjD, Sjögren’s disease; NCT, ClinicalTrials.gov identifier.
